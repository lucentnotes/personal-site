Title: What Stays — Act I: A Place Left Empty | Chinese Subtitles

Writing is part of my everyday life. For this project, I adapted my own fiction into images, sound and a few reader-controlled gestures, exploring another way to experience a story.

What Stays / 里面有人
Act I: A Place Left Empty / 空出来的位置

This is the linear video edition, with Chinese subtitles and no voiceover. The notebook interaction at the end is demonstrated automatically.
